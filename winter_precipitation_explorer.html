What's Happening in the Atmosphere? (Top-to-Bottom Journey)

1

Cloud Formation (3.0 km)

Inside the sub-freezing cloud (-9°C / 16°F), water vapor freezes directly onto microscopic particles to form delicate six-sided snowflakes.

2

Mid-Level Journey (2.2 – 0.5 km)

The entire mid-level air column remains below 0°C (32°F), so falling snowflakes never melt on their way down.

3

Near-Surface & Ground (0.0 km)

With the surface at -2°C (28°F), intact snowflakes settle gently onto the ground and accumulate as snowpack.
